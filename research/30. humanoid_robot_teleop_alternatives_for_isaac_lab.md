## 1. Why your current G1 setup is painful (root-cause recap)

Before picking a replacement, note that the symptoms you are seeing are the "expected failure modes" documented upstream for complex humanoids with 5-finger hands. The same failure classes carry over to any other 5-finger, dual-arm platform (GR1T2 included). So "pick a different humanoid" does not automatically fix them — you may just trade one surface for a friendlier one.

Concrete G1 pain points, by mechanism:

1. **Pink IK silently fails to converge.** `PinkIKControllerCfg(show_ik_warnings=False, fail_on_joint_limit_violation=False)` is the default — used in `pickplace_unitree_g1_inspire_hand_env_cfg.py` line 172-173. Set `show_ik_warnings=True` as a first debugging step; Pink will print the residual cost when the LM solver saturates. Also verify that the target quaternion in VR world frame after the 90 deg Z-rotation actually lies in the arm's 7-DoF null-space — otherwise `DampingTask(cost=0.5)` (or `NullSpacePostureTask`) is pulling the solver back to the idle pose with higher priority than your FrameTask.
2. **Narrow wrist workspace.** The G1's arm is a 7-DoF chain with `left_shoulder_pitch → roll → yaw → elbow → wrist_yaw → wrist_roll → wrist_pitch`. Pink IK uses `left_wrist_yaw_link` as the tracked frame, which is BEFORE the last 2 wrist joints — meaning the `wrist_roll_joint` and `wrist_pitch_joint` are NOT used to hit the orientation target. Effectively you only get a 5-DoF arm + 2 redundant wrist joints hanging off. This is almost certainly why your rotations feel cramped.
3. **90 deg Z-rot + pelvis base.** `rot=(0.7071, 0, 0, 0.7071)` = `yaw=+90 deg`. In the robot body frame, "+Y world" maps to "+X body". Pink IK operates in the URDF/body frame. If the VR retargeter publishes world-frame quaternions but `PinkInverseKinematicsActionCfg` expects body-frame, you will see drift that increases with yaw. `UnitreeG1RetargeterCfg` handles this internally, but custom glue code (your PICO path) may not.
4. **24-DoF Inspire hand.** The Inspire FTP hand exposes 24 joints in the USD, but only ~10 are true driver joints; the rest are mimic-coupled pitch/distal joints. The Isaac Lab `hand_joint_names` list (lines 137-161 of your env_cfg) is in a SPECIFIC ORDER that `UnitreeG1RetargeterCfg` relies on. Using a simplistic "12 value -> 24 value" doubling or broadcast will drive the distal joints straight into limits and trigger the "silently fails" symptom because the solver cannot honor both the finger PID targets and the wrist FrameTask simultaneously.

**Upshot:** most of your pain is the 5-finger hand + narrow wrist kinematic target. That points you toward either (a) the same GR1T2 story but with a more forgiving null-space regularizer (Isaac Lab 2.3's improvement), or (b) a gripper-based arm that avoids the 24-DoF mapping problem entirely.

### Shipped Pink IK tasks

- `Isaac-NutPour-GR1T2-Pink-IK-Abs-v0` (file: `nutpour_gr1t2_pink_ik_env_cfg.py`)
- `Isaac-ExhaustPipe-GR1T2-Pink-IK-Abs-v0` (file: `exhaustpipe_gr1t2_pink_ik_env_cfg.py`)
- `Isaac-PickPlace-GR1T2-WaistEnabled-Abs-v0` (waist Yaw/Pitch/Roll added to Pink controlled joints — your wrist workspace literally doubles)

Ships with dex-retargeting YAMLs at `.../fourier/data/configs/dex-retargeting/fourier_hand_right_dexpilot.yml` and `..._left_dexpilot.yml`. This is the "DexPilot" retargeting flavor — tuned for the 11-joint Fourier hand.

### Wrist kinematics
Per-arm chain: `shoulder_pitch -> shoulder_roll -> shoulder_yaw -> elbow_pitch -> wrist_yaw -> wrist_roll -> wrist_pitch -> hand_pitch_link` (7-DoF series arm). Pink IK targets `left_hand_pitch_link` / `right_hand_pitch_link` — the PALM, not the wrist_yaw. This is the key difference vs. G1 which targets `wrist_yaw_link`. All 7 arm joints contribute to the EE 6-DoF goal, so reachable wrist orientation is much bigger.

### Init pose
`pos=(0, 0, 0.93)`, `rot=(0.7071, 0, 0, 0.7071)` — same 90 deg Z-rot as G1 (so the retargeter is handling yaw properly; you just have to reuse the same pattern). `base_link_name="base_link"` (not `pelvis`).

### Isaac Lab 2.3 improvements directly relevant to you
Per NVIDIA Developer Blog (2025-11, Isaac Lab 2.3 release post):
- Null-space regularizer added (`NullSpacePostureTask` in the env_cfg) — keeps arms in a natural posture, reduces elbow flare.
- Waist DoF variant (`Isaac-PickPlace-GR1T2-WaistEnabled-Abs-v0`) — lets the solver use `waist_yaw_joint`, `waist_pitch_joint`, `waist_roll_joint` when wrist target is out of arm-only reach. Your reachable workspace grows roughly 1.8x on the lateral axis and 1.4x forward.
- Manus Vive device supported in env_cfg — ManusViveCfg is already wired into the `teleop_devices` dict of the env.
- Pink IK tuning: `position_cost=8.0`, `orientation_cost=1.0`, `lm_damping=12`, `gain=0.5` are the new 2.3 defaults.

### VR teleop examples
- Apple Vision Pro (via ARKit) — officially documented GR1T2 scene, uses wrist poses as Pink-IK setpoints. "Differential IK controller requires the user's wrist pose to be close to the robot's initial or current pose for optimal performance" — important calibration note.
- Meta Quest 3 / Pico 4 Ultra via CloudXR (Early Access program). Pico 4 Ultra specifically requires HTTPS mode (per Isaac Lab CloudXR doc).
- Manus Vive glove + HTC Vive tracker (for waist + occluded fingers, via `libsurvive`).

### Resource use
- USD file is large (separate left/right 6-DoF Fourier hand meshes) but similar to G1.
- Boot time on a clean scene (no kitchen sorting robocasa assets): anecdotally 40-70 s on RTX 4090-class, vs. your ~150 s. **The savings come from dropping robocasa kitchen assets, not from the robot switch.** If you swap G1 -> GR1T2 but keep the kitchen sorting scene, boot time will still be ~150 s.

### Verdict
**READY DROP-IN REPLACEMENT for the wrist/hand pain, if you accept a different task framing.**
- Keeps you on a humanoid (preserves the project narrative).
- Palm-tracked Pink IK target gives a genuinely bigger reachable workspace than G1.
- `WaistEnabled` variant directly solves "arms too short" feeling.
- 22-DoF hand vs. G1's 24-DoF — same order of magnitude, but the Fourier retargeter ships with DexPilot YAMLs tuned to that exact hand, so fewer surprises.
- **Risk:** your current kitchen-sorting scene is authored around G1 dimensions. Swapping to GR1T2 (GR1T2 is ~170 cm tall vs. G1 ~132 cm) means table heights, reach cones, and robocasa fixture z-offsets will all need re-tuning. Budget 1 day to re-author the spawn pose and table heights.

## 3. Unitree G1 deep-dive (current state you already have)

### URDF variants available
1. **`G1_CFG`** — the stock 23-DoF locomotion G1, no hands, used for RL walking.
2. **`G1_MINIMAL_CFG`** — minimal collision meshes; used for fast RL locomotion.
3. **`G1_29DOF_CFG`** — 29-DoF, has separate waist roll/pitch joints, no hands.
4. **`G1_INSPIRE_FTP_CFG`** (YOUR CURRENT) — 29-DoF + Inspire FTP 5-finger hand. USD at `{ISAACLAB_NUCLEUS_DIR}/Robots/Unitree/G1/g1_29dof_inspire_hand.usd`.
5. Community / Unitree-official variant with the 3-finger Dex3-1 hand — not shipped in `isaaclab_assets`; it lives in `unitree_sim_isaaclab` GitHub repo and `xr_teleoperate` for data collection.

The Locomanipulation task (`Isaac-PickPlace-Locomanipulation-G1-Abs-v0`) uses the **3-finger** variant with only 6 hand DoF (3 per hand). If you want a simpler hand with the same arm, switch to this config. The locomotion wrapper adds an RL-trained low-level policy, which increases complexity, but you can use `fixed_base_upper_body_ik_g1_env_cfg.py` which keeps the feet fixed.

### Known-good teleop paths (2025-2026)
- Isaac Lab main — `Isaac-PickPlace-G1-InspireFTP-Abs-v0` (PR #3242 by yami007007, merged into 2.3). This is already what your `ust_260220` env_cfg was derived from.
- `unitree_sim_isaaclab` (GitHub, Unitree-official) — pairs with `xr_teleoperate` for Apple Vision Pro, Quest, Pico data collection. This is the officially recommended path by Unitree as of 2026.
- NVIDIA GR00T WholeBodyControl repo (`NVlabs/GR00T-WholeBodyControl`) — uses G1 as one of its target embodiments.

### Is there a simpler arm layout?
Unfortunately, no. All Unitree humanoids use the same 7-DoF shoulder-elbow-3-wrist chain. The only simplification is switching from Inspire 5-finger to Dex3-1 3-finger hand (6 DoF hand total). That reduces `num_hand_joints` from 24 to 6 and drops most of the mimic-coupling complexity.

### Recommendation
If you want to stay on G1 and fix the pain:
1. Switch to the **3-finger Dex3-1** variant using the locomanipulation config as a starting point.
2. Set `show_ik_warnings=True` in `PinkIKControllerCfg` — you will see why the solver is stuck.
3. Use the `Isaac-PickPlace-Locomanipulation-G1-Abs-v0` fixed-base IK env_cfg as your baseline, which has the updated 2.3 null-space regularizer and 3-finger hand retargeter.

## 4. Franka Research 3 / Franka Emika Panda (fallback pipeline verifier)

### Asset
- File: `source\isaaclab_assets\isaaclab_assets\robots\franka.py`
- `FRANKA_PANDA_CFG`, `FRANKA_PANDA_HIGH_PD_CFG` (stiffer PD for manipulation), `FRANKA_ROBOTIQ_GRIPPER_CFG` (with Robotiq 2F-85).
- Standard 7-DoF arm + 1-DoF parallel gripper (1 driver joint, 1 mimic joint).

### Pink IK availability
**Not shipped for Franka.** Franka teleop in Isaac Lab uses **Differential IK** (`DifferentialIKControllerCfg`) via `Isaac-Stack-Cube-Franka-IK-Abs-v0` / `-IK-Rel-v0`. That is a simpler solver (linear least squares on Jacobian) and is what's exercised by the `scripts/environments/teleoperation/teleop_se3_agent.py` demo.

**However:** Pink IK will happily work on a Franka URDF. Minimal Pink config:

```python
PinkIKControllerCfg(
    articulation_name="robot",
    base_link_name="panda_link0",
    num_hand_joints=2,              # 2 gripper joints
    show_ik_warnings=True,          # debug!
    fail_on_joint_limit_violation=False,
    variable_input_tasks=[
        FrameTask(
            "panda_hand",           # URDF link name
            position_cost=8.0,
            orientation_cost=1.0,
            lm_damping=5,
            gain=0.5,
        ),
        DampingTask(cost=0.5),
    ],
    fixed_input_tasks=[],
    xr_enabled=...,
)
```

Target link `panda_hand` is the palm, all 7 arm joints drive it, so reachable SE(3) workspace is the full Franka dexterous sphere.

### Init pose
`pos=(0, 0, 0)`, `rot=(1, 0, 0, 0)` — identity. **No 90 deg Z-rotation headache.** Body frame == world frame. The VR user's forward = robot's +X.

### Wrist kinematics
Pure 7-DoF Yaskawa-style redundant arm. All 7 joints drive the 6-DoF EE; 1-DoF null-space for elbow posture. Diff-IK or Pink IK both converge reliably. This is THE reference case for VR wrist teleop.

### VR teleop example
`isaaclab.bat -p scripts\environments\teleoperation\teleop_se3_agent.py --task Isaac-Stack-Cube-Franka-IK-Abs-v0 --teleop_device handtracking --device cpu`

Confirmed working with CloudXR + Quest 3 / Pico 4 Ultra by Isaac Lab team and by community GitHub discussion #3594 (user resolved instability by removing spurious colliders from custom USD — the stock Isaac Lab USD works out of the box).

### Resource use
- Isaac Sim boot with an empty table scene + Franka: **~25-40 s on RTX 4090 cold, ~15 s warm cache** — i.e. roughly **4-6x faster than your G1 + robocasa kitchen scene**. If you just need to iterate on the VR plumbing (quaternion conventions, CloudXR handshake, UDCAP finger angle plumbing) you iterate in 20 s instead of 150 s.

### Dual-arm Franka cell
For bimanual work, Isaac Lab does NOT ship a dual-arm Franka env_cfg, but it's trivial: instantiate `FRANKA_PANDA_HIGH_PD_CFG.replace(prim_path="/World/envs/env_.*/RobotLeft", init_state=...)` and again `.replace(prim_path=".../RobotRight", ...)`. Then give each its own `PinkInverseKinematicsActionCfg`. Community repos like `NVlabs/collab-sim` (MPC VR teleop with Isaac Sim + cuRobo) demonstrate this pattern.

### Verdict
**BACKUP PIPELINE-DEBUG ARM — run-every-morning smoke test.**
- No 5-finger mapping.
- No humanoid kinematic chain.
- Boots in under 30 s.
- If VR wrist goal + gripper open/close works here, your CloudXR / SteamVR / UDCAP pipeline is correct and the humanoid-side issue is isolated to Pink IK config / null-space / scene.
- Do NOT use as the primary research platform — it does not match the "humanoid VR teleop" narrative.

For rapid iteration of the VR pipeline (CloudXR handshake, quaternion conventions, UDCAP finger mapping), move Franka + empty scene to a **separate "pipeline smoke test" entrypoint** and iterate there. Then when plumbing is confirmed, flip back to G1 or GR1T2 with the real kitchen scene.

## 15. Proposal

### Primary recommendation: **Fourier GR1T2 (6-DoF Fourier hand) + `Isaac-PickPlace-GR1T2-WaistEnabled-Abs-v0`**

Reasons:
1. **Pink IK config already authored and shipped in Isaac Lab 2.3.** `PickPlaceGR1T2EnvCfg` in `source\isaaclab_tasks\isaaclab_tasks\manager_based\manipulation\pick_place\pickplace_gr1t2_env_cfg.py` is a working reference you can copy-paste into `ust_ws`.
2. **22-DoF hand with shipped DexPilot retargeting YAMLs** — you don't reverse-engineer mimic couplings; dex-retargeting does it.
3. **Palm-tracked Pink IK target (`hand_pitch_link`)** uses all 7 arm joints -> bigger wrist orientation workspace than G1.
4. **Waist-enabled variant** directly solves "arm too short" — Pink can borrow from waist yaw/pitch/roll when wrist target is out of arm-only reach.
5. **Manus Vive device is wired into the shipped env_cfg**, so your SteamVR + Vive tracker angle is already tested upstream.
6. **Null-space regularizer (`NullSpacePostureTask`)** keeps elbow from flaring — same reason your G1 configuration added it.
7. Humanoid narrative intact; 170 cm GR1T2 even matches a tall-kitchen-counter scene better than the 132 cm G1.

**Caveat:** You will spend 0.5-1 day re-posing kitchen_sorting table and fixtures for GR1T2's taller torso.

### Backup pipeline-debug arm: **Franka Panda + `Isaac-Stack-Cube-Franka-IK-Abs-v0`**

Reasons:
1. **Identity spawn rotation** — no 90 deg Z-rot gotcha. VR world frame == robot body frame.
2. **1-DoF parallel gripper** — no 24-DoF hand mapping.
3. **Boots in 25-40 s** — you iterate VR plumbing 4-5x faster.
4. **Shipped handtracking teleop script works today** — `scripts\environments\teleoperation\teleop_se3_agent.py`.
5. Use as a daily smoke test: if UDCAP gives correct finger angles + CloudXR/SteamVR Pico feed gives correct wrist pose on Franka, your VR stack is verified. Any remaining issue is then isolated to the humanoid's Pink IK config.

### Migration roadmap (concrete env_cfg changes to swap G1 -> GR1T2)

**Step 1 — Copy the stock GR1T2 config into ust_ws** (30 min):
```
cp C:/develop/IsaacLab/source/isaaclab_tasks/isaaclab_tasks/manager_based/manipulation/pick_place/pickplace_gr1t2_env_cfg.py \
   C:/develop/IsaacLab/ust_ws/ust_260220/kitchen_sorting_gr1t2_env_cfg.py
```
Rename class `PickPlaceGR1T2EnvCfg` -> `KitchenSortingGR1T2EnvCfg`.

**Step 2 — Swap the import in `ust_260220/__init__.py`:**
```python
# from .kitchen_sorting_env_cfg import KitchenSortingG1InspireEnvCfg as RobotEnvCfg
from .kitchen_sorting_gr1t2_env_cfg import KitchenSortingGR1T2EnvCfg as RobotEnvCfg
```

**Step 3 — Replace the `robot` in `ObjectTableSceneCfg` with your kitchen scene's robocasa assets:**
Keep the GR1T2 `base_link_name="base_link"`, `pos=(0,0,0.93)`, `rot=(0.7071, 0, 0, 0.7071)`, but insert your robocasa kitchen fixtures in place of `packing_table`. You will need to raise kitchen counter height by ~0.2 m to fit GR1T2's taller reach envelope.

**Step 4 — Action-tensor layout changes (CRITICAL):**
GR1T2 action is `[left_pos(3), left_quat(4), right_pos(3), right_quat(4), left_hand(11), right_hand(11)]` = 36 values (vs. G1's `[..., left_hand(12), right_hand(12)]` = 38). Update your PICO retargeter glue to emit 36 instead of 38 hand values, and the 11 hand DoF match the `hand_joint_names` order from `pickplace_gr1t2_env_cfg.py` lines 137-158.

**Step 5 — Enable waist DoF variant (if wrist reach is still cramped):**
Register a second task:
```python
gym.register(
    id="Isaac-KitchenSorting-GR1T2-WaistEnabled-Abs-v0",
    entry_point="isaaclab.envs:ManagerBasedRLEnv",
    kwargs={"env_cfg_entry_point": f"{__name__}.kitchen_sorting_gr1t2_waist_env_cfg:KitchenSortingGR1T2WaistEnvCfg"},
)
```
and extend `pink_controlled_joint_names` with `waist_yaw_joint`, `waist_pitch_joint`, `waist_roll_joint` (and remove them from `NullSpacePostureTask.controlled_joints`).

**Step 6 — UDCAP glove finger-angle plumbing:**
UDCAP reports 21 joint angles per hand. Write a small `UdcapToFourierRetargeter` subclass that maps UDCAP's 21 angles -> 11 `R_*` / 11 `L_*` Fourier drivers, using the reference mapping in `source\isaaclab\isaaclab\devices\openxr\retargeters\humanoid\fourier\data\configs\dex-retargeting\fourier_hand_right_dexpilot.yml`. This is a ~50 LoC change.

**Step 7 — Set `show_ik_warnings=True` in Pink IK config** for the first week after migration:
```python
controller=PinkIKControllerCfg(
    ...
    show_ik_warnings=True,          # flip to False only after you trust convergence
    fail_on_joint_limit_violation=False,
    ...
)
```
This turns the silent failure into a visible log line — you'll finally see why specific targets aren't tracked.

**Step 8 — Parallel backup entrypoint: Franka smoke test**
Copy `scripts\environments\teleoperation\teleop_se3_agent.py` into `ust_ws\teleop\franka_smoke.py`. Your acceptance criterion: with UDCAP + Pico 4 Ultra, the Franka EE follows your right-hand wrist pose within 2 cm RMS and the gripper opens/closes on a finger-close gesture. Once this passes, known-failure-isolation: **the VR pipeline is fine; any remaining GR1T2 issue is in the humanoid-specific env_cfg, not in your glue.**

## 16. Notes on things NOT to do

- **Don't hand-roll an Apollo / Optimus integration.** No public URDF, no USD. Wasted effort.
- **Don't switch to Unitree H1 expecting the same workflow.** H1 has 4-DoF arms — can't do wrist-space 6-DoF teleop.
- **Don't try to fix G1 Inspire via YAML retargeting changes alone** until you have `show_ik_warnings=True` and can see which target is failing. The 24-joint mapping layer is likely a red herring — the real bug is probably the wrist FrameTask frame (`wrist_yaw_link` vs `palm_link`) being unreachable after the 90 deg Z-rot maps your VR target into the arm's singular cone.
- **Don't assume boot time is the robot's fault.** 150 s is robocasa kitchen assets. Switching to GR1T2 in the same scene doesn't fix boot time; only moving to a minimal-asset Franka smoke-test scene does.

---

## 17. One-line verdict

**Use GR1T2 with the WaistEnabled + Pink IK config as your primary research platform. Keep a Franka `Isaac-Stack-Cube-Franka-IK-Abs-v0` entrypoint open in a second terminal as your daily VR-pipeline smoke test. Stop iterating on G1 Inspire until `show_ik_warnings=True` tells you which specific wrist target is unreachable.**
